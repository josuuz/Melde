# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (chosen by the user). Design tokens live in CSS custom properties so the system ports to a future e-commerce platform (Shopify, Nuvemshop, Next or Astro) without rework.

## Users

Young Brazilian adults who want something quick and delicious to put on banana, bread/toast, pancakes, oats or yogurt, and people who eat something before training. They know doce de leite by heart and reach for it as the default creamy spread. The first visitor arrives from social/word of mouth, on a phone, deciding in seconds whether this is worth trying.

## Product Purpose

Meldê is a new brand of creamed honey (mel cremoso) positioned as a modern alternative to doce de leite. The first product is MELDÊ ORIGINAL: mel cremoso, 100% mel, 250 g jar. This first site is a brand landing page that presents the brand and validates demand through a waitlist; success is people joining the list. The e-commerce (catalog, cart, checkout) comes next and will be built on this design system.

## Positioning

"Mel, só que cremoso." 100% honey in a creamy, spreadable texture that occupies the territory people associate with doce de leite. The desire trigger is the TEXTURE: glossy, thick, pulled by a spoon. Food brand premium + lifestyle + performance + editorial; not artisanal-farm honey, not natural-store cliché, not a supplement/gym brand.

## Operating Context

Eaten with banana, pão/torrada, panqueca, aveia, iogurte, and before training (pré-treino). Sold in a short, wide-mouth jar: transparent body showing the texture, deep brown lid, minimal label.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only product now: MELDÊ ORIGINAL, mel cremoso, 100% mel, 250 g.
- Future line MELDÊ PROTEIN (with whey) may only appear as a small teaser ("em breve"), never as a main product.
- No nutritional, medical or sports-performance claims that cannot be proven. "100% mel" is the only composition claim.
- CTA mechanism: waitlist form (name + WhatsApp or e-mail). Submission endpoint is undecided; the form is built ready to plug into Formspree / Google Forms / a sheet.
- Undecided: price, sales channels, launch date, domain, social handles, contact details.

## Brand Commitments

- Name written in lowercase: meldê. The "ê" and its circumflex are the brand signature; the circumflex may very subtly evoke wings, a drop or hexagonal geometry. Never a drawn bee next to the logo.
- Palette given by the user: Mel dourado #E5A52A, Creme #FFF3D6, Marrom profundo #30251E, Branco #FFFFFF (derived auxiliary tones allowed). Avoid an excessively beige/"organic" look.
- Copy lines supplied by the user: "Mel, só que cremoso." / "Energia em outra textura." / "Seu pré começa aqui." / "Espalha. Mistura. Mergulha." / "100% mel. Outra experiência." / "Não é doce de leite. É Meldê." / "Do pote pro treino." / "Seu pré pode ser mais gostoso." / "Tem algo a mais sendo misturado." / "Prova uma colher. Depois a gente conversa."
- Avoid: honeycombs and bees as decoration, farm/rustic cues, gym/maromba cues, SaaS cards.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference images in the project root (direction only, never to copy): two doce de leite texture shots (spoon pulling a glossy dollop) and a chocolate brand landing (large product hero, huge type, asymmetric colored blocks).
- No real product photography, testimonials, prices, retailers or press exist yet. Imagery in this phase is authored 3D render labeled as direction, to be replaced by a real shoot. Never fabricate reviews, prices, stockists or health claims.

## Product Principles

1. Texture first: every surface should make people want to taste it.
2. Honest and short: 100% mel, nothing promised that cannot be proven.
3. Everyday ritual over fitness niche: breakfast, snack and pre-training are equal moments.
4. Desire over decoration: photography, typography and composition carry the brand, not ornaments.
5. Built to grow: this landing is the seed of the e-commerce system.
