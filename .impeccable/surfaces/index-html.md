# Surface brief: landing Meldê (index.html)

Scope: brand launch landing for MELDÊ ORIGINAL. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: young Brazilian adults, phone first, who know doce de leite by heart; breakfast, snack and pre-training eaters.
Job: understand "mel, só que cremoso" in seconds, want the texture, join the waitlist.
Action: waitlist form (nome + WhatsApp ou e-mail), endpoint pluggable later.
Proof/content: 100% mel (single ingredient), 250 g, uses (banana, pão, panqueca, iogurte, aveia, pré-treino). Photography is AI-generated/authored direction imagery, to be replaced by a real shoot. No prices, stockists, reviews or health/sport claims.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, tokens in CSS custom properties, light on common phones, few excellent animations, reduced-motion safe. Future e-commerce builds on this system.
Memorable moment: the jar's front label at wall scale with the texture showing through its window, and one continuous honey thread that runs from the hero spoon to the final CTA.
Unresolved: waitlist endpoint, social handles, domain, contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the Meldê jar label unrolled flat. Every section is a label panel (frente, textura, modo de usar, composição, pré, produto, lote futuro, lacre), and rounded windows cut into the label show the creamy texture behind the glass. It refuses rustic honey (kraft, hexagons, bees, serif on cream) and the chocolate-drip landing (drips framing colored blocks).

OWN-WORLD: Two grounds: label white #FFFFFF and lid brown #30251E, with lid-shadow #1E1712. Mel dourado #E5A52A is light, reserved for product, photography and action; creamed honey #F2CF7E and amber #9A5B12 give it depth; creme #FFF3D6 only as window tint. Components: panels with lot codes (P-01..P-08), hairline die-cut rules, jar-shaped rounded windows, net-weight numerals, a lacre seal band as divider, seal-shaped CTA. The circumflex is the only icon primitive (arrows, bullets, separators). Type: custom rounded lowercase wordmark; one grotesk family with a width axis, expanded black caps for display, normal width for text, semi-expanded tracked caps for label microtype.

STORY: Visitor sees the jar and a spoon pulling a glossy fold, reads "Mel, só que cremoso.", learns it is 100% mel and spreadable, finds their moment (com o quê?), sees pre-training as one ritual among many, meets a product that could sit on a shelf tomorrow, notices something being mixed, joins the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White label panel. Top strip is the lid band: brown, wordmark small left, lot code, nav, "quero experimentar" seal right. Headline "MEL, SÓ QUE CREMOSO." in expanded black caps, top-left, about 11vw. The labeled jar photo dominates the right half, bottom cut by the viewport, overlapping a jar-shaped window that shows the macro spoon pull. Bottom-left: one-line sub, the seal CTA in gold, and the "100% MEL · 250 g" net-weight callout. The honey thread leaves the spoon and runs down out of frame.

FORM: The label unrolled (FMCG pack architecture), position 3 on my ordered list of 7, seed key 4b01c50b. Raises: one continuous thread (neon), reserved gold light (understory), participatory moment picker (teen quiz), 1:12 scale jumps (Merz), panel lot codes (teletext). Signature interaction: the honey thread draws itself with scroll and the moments picker swaps photo and line. Motion grammar: slow, viscous ease-out (cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.1,1)), 600 to 1100 ms, hero entrance once, text reveals by line, parallax under 6 percent.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
